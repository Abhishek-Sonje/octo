How it works

Zero infrastructure. One binary.

01 —

Spawn a shell

octo starts bash or zsh in a PTY — exactly like your terminal does. Full ANSI, colours, interactive programs.

02 —

Open a tunnel

The binary connects outbound to the octo relay. No firewall rules, no port forwarding, no VPN.

03 —

Share the link

Send the URL to anyone. They open it in a browser — xterm.js renders a full terminal. Nothing to install.

04 —

Close when done

Ctrl+C kills the session and the tunnel. Nothing is persisted. Nothing is logged on the relay.
